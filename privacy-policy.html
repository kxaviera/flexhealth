1. Information We Collect

When you shop with Flex Health, we may collect:

  • Name, phone number, email address, and delivery address
  • Order history and payment method (we do not store full card details)
  • Device and browser information for site performance and security
  • Communications when you contact our support team

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Send order updates, delivery notifications, and support responses
  • Improve our website, products, and customer experience
  • Send promotional offers if you have subscribed to our newsletter (you may unsubscribe anytime)

3. Sharing & Security

We share information only with trusted partners necessary to fulfill orders — payment processors, courier partners, and IT service providers — under strict confidentiality agreements.

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

4. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us. You can opt out of marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link or by writing to us.
