1. Order Cancellation

You may cancel your order before it is shipped by contacting us at +91 924 650 1017 or sales@flexhealth.in with your order details.

Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may not be possible. In such cases, please refer to our Return Policy.

2. Refund Eligibility

Refunds are processed for eligible returns including:

  • Wrong product delivered
  • Damaged or defective items reported within 48 hours of delivery
  • Sealed, unopened products returned as per return policy

Opened supplement tubs, used products, or items without original packaging may not qualify for a full refund.

3. Refund Process

  • Approved refunds are initiated within 5–7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned product.
  • Refunds are credited to the original payment method (UPI, card, or bank transfer).
  • For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds are processed via bank transfer or UPI after verification.
  • Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

4. Cash on Delivery Refunds

For COD orders, please share your bank account or UPI details when initiating a return. Refunds typically take 7–10 business days after approval.

5. Need Help?

Email sales@flexhealth.in or call +91 924 650 1017 for refund assistance.
