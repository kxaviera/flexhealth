1. Return Window

Eligible products may be returned within 7 days of delivery. Please inspect your order upon receipt and contact us immediately if there is an issue.

2. Eligible Returns

  • Product received is incorrect or different from what was ordered
  • Product arrived damaged or with a broken seal (report within 48 hours with photos)
  • Unopened, sealed products in original packaging with batch label intact

3. Non-Returnable Items

  • Opened or partially used supplement containers
  • Products without original packaging, batch number, or expiry label
  • Clearance or final-sale items marked as non-returnable
  • Products returned after the 7-day window

4. How to Return

  1. Contact us at sales@flexhealth.in or +91 924 650 1017 with your order ID and reason for return.
  2. Our team will confirm eligibility and provide return instructions.
  3. Pack the product securely in its original box where possible.
  4. Once received and inspected, we will process your refund or exchange per our Refund Policy.
