1. Free Shipping

Flex Health offers free shipping on all orders delivered across India. Orders are packed securely to protect supplement products during transit.

2. Delivery Timelines

  • Hyderabad & Telangana: 2–4 business days
  • Metro cities: 3–6 business days
  • Other locations: 5–10 business days

Delivery times may vary during festivals, sales events, or due to courier delays beyond our control. You will receive SMS/email updates once your order is shipped.

3. Cash on Delivery (COD)

Cash on Delivery is available on eligible orders across most pin codes in India. Pay the courier when your package arrives.

  • Please keep exact change ready where possible
  • Inspect the package before payment — do not accept visibly damaged shipments
  • COD orders may require phone verification before dispatch
  • A small COD handling fee may apply on select orders (shown at checkout)

4. Order Tracking

Track your order anytime with your Order ID and mobile number on our Track Order page. Once shipped, you may also receive updates via SMS or email.

Need help? Contact us with your order number.
